# Open Problems and Questions

Date: 2026-07-23  
Scope: `w17-3d-codex` mechanical repository only.

This register consolidates the unresolved problems, conflicts, ambiguities, owner
decisions, physical checks, and load-bearing **ASSUMPTIONS** carried by the existing
fit studies, measurement/risk/decision registers, evidence tables, assembly steps,
and validation gates. It does not change any source value, analysis, STL, gate,
placement, recommendation, or conclusion.

Resolution ownership is explicit:

- **(a) CODEX measurement** — further read-only STL/slicer/drawing/CAD inspection that
  can be done in this repository; it is not an owner question.
- **(b) OWNER physical/ASM check** — requires the real parts, assembled car, printer,
  gauges, scales, or powered bench.
- **(c) OWNER decision/ground truth** — requires the selected SKU/datasheet, intended
  use, priorities, or permission; geometry cannot answer it.

Severity means: **BLOCKER** stops the named next CAD/print/assembly gate;
**HIGH** can invalidate a major subsystem or safety/service plan; **MEDIUM** causes
local redesign or feature loss; **LOW** is optional or reserve scope. Rows are sorted
BLOCKER-first. “Confidence” describes the current assumption, not the seriousness of
the problem.
